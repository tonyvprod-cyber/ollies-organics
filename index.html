Meet Ollie
The Root

Named for the woman whose heart was steeped in compassion

Ollie's Organics carries my late mother's name because her heart was steeped in compassion, and her only real desire in this life was to serve. I had the honor of caring for her through her final years.

"Tea was one of the few things that reliably brought her peace — a small, steady ritual that eased her, even on the hardest days."

Her radiant spirit is where this began. What started as a way to comfort my own mother became a lifestyle, and eventually a business — a way to keep extending that same love, one nourishing cup at a time.

Held, Until Her Last Breath

This is what caring for her actually looked like

Not a polished photograph — a real one. Her hand cupping my face, steadying me the way she always had, even as the caregiving had long since reversed. I couldn't hold back the tears. She could still hold mine.

That closeness is the whole reason Ollie's Organics exists: a business built on the belief that the smallest rituals of comfort are worth protecting, and worth sharing.

The Hands That Built This

A caregiver first. A founder because of it.

She's a wife, a mother of three, and — for years — the one holding everything together: a full-time job, a household, and the care of both of her aging parents, including her mother, Ollie, through to the very end.

It was in that season, reading every label on everything she fed to the people she loved most, that she started noticing what was actually hiding in their food. Her mother's example — a woman who served others until her last breath — was the reason she went looking for something better.

That search led her to natural remedies, and eventually to a certification in nutrition and health. She's been on that path for 38 years. Ollie's Organics is that path, finally offered to you.

— Rooted in love, from our family to yours
